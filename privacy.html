Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

StreamClipper ("the app") is a single-user desktop tool operated by the owner of the Devil Exxon channel. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by its operator to post to the operator's own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It has no public sign-up and no other users.

Data the app accesses

Google / YouTube: with the operator's consent, the app receives an OAuth token with the youtube.upload scope. It uses it only to upload and schedule videos on the operator's own channel. It does not read, store or share any other YouTube or Google account data.

TikTok: with the operator's consent, the app receives an OAuth token with the user.info.basic and video.publish scopes. It uses them only to read the operator's posting settings and to publish videos to the operator's own account.

Stream content: the app downloads public stream recordings and their public chat replays from creators who have given permission to be clipped. Chat messages are used only as anonymous per-second activity counts to find highlight moments. They are not published or shared.

Storage and security

All data, including OAuth tokens, videos and analysis files, is stored only on the operator's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server operated by StreamClipper; none exists. Data is sent only to YouTube and TikTok, as part of the uploads described above.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share any user data with third parties.

Third-party services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies. TikTok's use of data is governed by the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page and in TikTok's app settings under "Security & permissions". Deleting the app's folder on the operator's computer removes all stored data and tokens.
